Find openly licensed photographs

Searches Openverse (Wikimedia Commons, Flickr and others) for pictures you may use commercially and change: CC0, public domain, CC BY and CC BY-SA. Adding one keeps its creator, licence and link. Check each page before you rely on it.

Train a style

A style teaches HiDream-O1 or Qwen-Image-Edit the look of your references: the light, the places, the framing. It needs at least 5 pictures you may use, ideally 20 to 60, each with a caption that describes it. FLUX 3 isn't offered here: its free licence does not cover training.
